\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The SolarWinds campaign worked because the attackers did not need to break a defence. They used the supplier's build and signing process, and customers installed the result with every check passing. The lab reproduces that trust failure, with a build that accepts a higher public dependency, and the check-in sequence that followed. It stops after the check-in sequence: no second stage, no lateral movement, and no real collection, because the \texttt{collect} command reads a decoy file.

A signature identifies the signer. Build isolation, provenance records, and independent verification act before the signature exists, while DNS and network monitoring act after installation. The papers support both layers, and none of the six tests them together in a lab like this one, which is what the research question proposes to do.
