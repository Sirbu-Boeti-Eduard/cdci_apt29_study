Finding (source-reported): ESET reported that Operation Ghost began in 2013 and remained active when its researchers published their findings in 2019. ESET linked the campaign to the Dukes (APT29) and reported targeting foreign ministries in at least three European countries and an EU member state's embassy in Washington, D.C.

References:
- [ESET, "Operation Ghost Hunt: The DNC hacking group 'Dukes' still attacks government targets, ESET discovers"](https://www.eset.com/us/about/newsroom/press-releases/operation-ghost-hunt-the-dnc-hacking-group-dukes-still-attacks-government-targets-eset-discovers-1/), 17 October 2019, opening paragraphs.

Finding (source-reported): CrowdStrike reported two distinct Russian-affiliated actors on the Democratic National Committee (DNC) network: Cozy Bear, associated with APT29, was present from summer 2015; Fancy Bear, associated with APT28, breached the network separately in April 2016. CrowdStrike said it had identified no collaboration or mutual awareness between the two actors.

References:
- [Dmitri Alperovitch, CrowdStrike, "Bears in the Midst: Intrusion Into the Democratic National Committee"](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/blog/bears-midst-intrusion-democratic-national-committee/), 14 June 2016, reproduced in CrowdStrike's 4 June 2020 retrospective, embedded section "Bears in the Midst: Intrusion Into the Democratic National Committee," final paragraphs.

Finding (source-reported): In July 2020, the UK National Cyber Security Centre and Canada's Communications Security Establishment reported that APT29 had targeted organizations involved in COVID-19 vaccine development in Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom. They assessed that the likely objective was to obtain information and intellectual property about vaccine development and testing.

References:
- [UK National Cyber Security Centre, "Advisory: APT29 targets COVID-19 vaccine development"](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/news/advisory-apt29-targets-covid-19-vaccine-development), 16 July 2020, introduction, paragraphs beginning "The group uses" and "Throughout 2020."
